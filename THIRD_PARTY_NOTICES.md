## Demonstration photograph
The NASA astronaut portrait is distributed in scikit-image's sample data. Its documentation identifies the source as NASA and states that it is public domain:
https://scikit-image.org/docs/stable/api/skimage.data.html#skimage.data.astronaut

Downloaded image:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/scikit-image/scikit-image/v0.25.2/skimage/data/astronaut.png

The sample hash is recorded in `docs/results/demo.json`. The photograph is used only to demonstrate person detection. No NASA endorsement or industrial safety validation is implied. Orange polygons are an explicitly illustrative configuration; green boxes and confidence come from YOLO.
